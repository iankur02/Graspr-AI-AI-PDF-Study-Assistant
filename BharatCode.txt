PROJECT OVERVIEW:
Grapsr AI Study Assistant is an AI-powered learning platform designed to help students study more efficiently using their own educational materials.

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Students often spend significant time reading lengthy PDFs, preparing revision notes, creating practice questions, and searching for specific information in study materials.

SOLUTION:
Grapsr AI Study Assistant provides an interactive platform that helps students understand and revise their study materials through AI-powered features.

KEY FEATURES:
- Upload and process PDF study materials.
- Generate concise summaries of educational content.
- Create practice questions and MCQs.
- Interact with study materials through AI-powered conversations.
- Organize learning materials and support self-paced study.
